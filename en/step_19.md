## Make the contact bands

Now that the complete game works with bare leads, build the finger contacts.

> [!TASK]
>
> Cut four strips of scrap paper or thin card. Wrap one loosely around your thumb and one around each of your index, middle, and ring fingers. Fasten each strip to itself so you can remove the band immediately.

> [!TASK]
>
> Add a conductive tape patch to the outside of each band. Extend the tape onto a flat paper tab where a crocodile clip can grip it. Label the finger tabs `1`, `2`, and `3`.

**Test:** Put on and remove every band. Check that they remain loose and that the three numbered patches can each touch the thumb patch comfortably.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> Keep conductive adhesive and crocodile clip jaws off your skin. If a band feels tight, remove it and make a larger one.
