### You will need

- A BBC micro:bit V2
- A USB data cable
- A battery pack and two AAA batteries
- Four crocodile or alligator clip leads
- Kitchen foil
- Thin card, such as a cereal box or a piece of cardboard
- Three or four elastic bands or hair bands
- Scissors
