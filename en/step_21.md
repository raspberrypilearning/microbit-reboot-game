## Connect the finger controller

Attach the tested leads to the four paper contacts.

> [!TASK]
>
> Keep the battery disconnected. Reconnect the four leads between the micro:bit and the paper tabs: `GND` to the thumb, `P0` to finger `1`, `P1` to finger `2`, and `P2` to finger `3`.
>
> Grip the conductive tape on each paper tab, not the band around your finger.

> [!TASK]
>
> Connect the battery pack and press button A. Watch each signal, then tap the matching numbered finger contact against the thumb contact. Release the contacts after every tap.

**Test:** Keep playing until each numbered finger has appeared at least once, then complete a five-term reboot. Each finger contact should behave like its tested crocodile lead, and the wrist fastening should remain comfortable and secure throughout the game.

> [!SAVE]
