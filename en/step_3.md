## Connect the first test circuit

Connect two crocodile leads before building anything for your fingers.

> [!TASK]
>
> Disconnect the USB cable and battery pack from the micro:bit. Connect one crocodile lead to `GND` and another to `P0`.

> [!TASK]
>
> Arrange the leads so their free metal jaws do not touch. Check that the jaws on the micro:bit grip only their own rings and do not touch `3V` or a neighbouring pin.

**Test:** Gently move both leads. They should stay attached to `P0` and `GND`, while the two free jaws remain separate.
