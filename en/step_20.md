## Attach the micro:bit to your wrist

Use a simple band or strap to hold the micro:bit while you play.

> [!TASK]
>
> Disconnect the battery pack. Use your band or strap to secure the micro:bit to your wrist. Position it with the LED display and buttons visible and the edge connector accessible.

> [!TASK]
>
> Move your hand gently and bend your wrist. Adjust the fastening if the board slides, feels uncomfortable, or stops you reaching the buttons and edge connector.

**Test:** Hold your arm in a comfortable playing position for 30 seconds. The fastening should not feel tight, and the micro:bit should stay secure without covering its display, buttons, or rings.
